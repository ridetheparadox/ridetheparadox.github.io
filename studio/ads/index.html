    Photoreal scenes, people and products from the latest video and image models, including Seedance 2.5, directed shot by shot.

04 / Questions

Good to
know.

Can you make an ad for my kind of business?

Almost certainly. If it can be shown, it can be made: food, retail, beauty, fitness, property, hospitality, apps, events and services.

Will it look like AI?

The goal is ads that look like real footage of real places and people. Every shot is reviewed and redone until it holds up.

Can you use my products, logo and photos?

Yes. Your own photos, logo and brand colours make the result more accurate and more yours.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually once the scope is clear. Send the form and you will get a private quote by email.

How long does it take, and what do I get?

Timing depends on the brief and is agreed before production. You get the finished ad in the formats you need, with one revision round included.

Is the use of AI disclosed?

Yes. Paradox works openly with AI. Read the AI disclosure for how imagery is made and labelled.
