Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 21 AUGUST 2026

This policy explains what information Paradox collects, why, and what choices you have. We collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Information you submit. If you use the inquiry form, we receive the name, email address, and message you type. That is the only personal information we actively collect.
  • Consent record. Your acceptance of the entry gate, and your cookie choice, are stored locally in your own browser. They are not transmitted to us.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may automatically log IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps for security and reliability.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. We do not collect payment information through this site.

How we use it

Only to respond to your inquiry, to operate and secure the site, and to comply with law. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Who we share it with

We use Formspree to deliver inquiry-form submissions to our email. Your submission passes through their systems under their own privacy policy. We otherwise do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

How long we keep it

Inquiry emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable business records, then deleted on request.

Your choices

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To make a request, email ridetheparadox@gmail.com and we will respond within the time the law allows. We may need to verify your identity first.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International visitors

The site is operated from the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, your information is processed in the United States.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
