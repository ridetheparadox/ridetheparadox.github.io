Cookie Policy

LAST UPDATED 24 SEPTEMBER 2026

This site stores your choices locally. If you choose Allow analytics, it also uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure site visits and link choices. We do not use advertising pixels.

What we store

  • Essential — consent record. When you accept the entry gate, we save that choice in your browser's local storage so you are not asked on every visit. It contains no personal information and is never sent to a server.
  • Essential — cookie preference. Your Allow analytics / Essential only choice, saved the same way.
  • Optional — GA4 analytics. After you choose Allow analytics, GA4 may set first-party _ga cookies to distinguish visits and sessions. It receives page views and site interactions, including which portal link you choose. Choosing Essential only leaves GA4 unloaded.

We do not load GA4 before analytics consent, use Meta Pixel, or send enquiry form fields to GA4. Analytics is configured without Google advertising signals or ad personalization.

Third parties

If you allow analytics, Google receives GA4 measurement data to provide reports to Paradox. See the Privacy Policy for the data involved. The inquiry form is delivered by Formspree, which may set its own cookies when a form is submitted. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving the request. Clicking through to other sites takes you to platforms with their own tracking.

Your control

Choose Essential only or Allow analytics in the cookie panel. You can change that choice using Cookie settings in the site footer. If you change to Essential only, the site stops sending GA4 events and removes its first-party _ga cookies on this site. You can also clear this site's stored data in your browser; this will reset both the cookie choice and the film acknowledgement.
