Questions

What does an AI video production company do?
It plans, generates and finishes video with AI image and video models instead of a film shoot. PARADOX directs every shot, redoes anything that does not hold up, then adds captions, logo and colour so the result looks like one finished campaign.
Can you work as a white-label partner for my agency?
Yes. Agency partnerships cover pitch visuals, campaign assets and white-label delivery, with your client relationship kept at the centre.
How much does it cost?
Every project is quoted individually once the scope is clear. Send the enquiry form and you get a private quote by email, with scope, fees and timings agreed before production.
Is the use of AI disclosed?
Yes. PARADOX works openly with AI, and all imagery is labelled as AI-generated. The AI disclosure page explains how the work is made and labelled.
Where do you work?
Worldwide and fully remote. Briefs, reviews and delivery all happen online.
