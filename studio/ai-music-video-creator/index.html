Questions

Can an AI music video match my song?
Yes. The visual direction is agreed with you first, then shots are planned around the mood and structure of the track and cut to the music in the edit.
Do I own the finished video?
Usage rights are set out in the quote before production, so you know exactly where the video can run.
How much does it cost?
Every project is quoted individually once the scope is clear. Send the enquiry form and you get a private quote by email, with scope, fees and timings agreed before production.
Is the use of AI disclosed?
Yes. PARADOX works openly with AI, and all imagery is labelled as AI-generated. The AI disclosure page explains how the work is made and labelled.
Where do you work?
Worldwide and fully remote. Briefs, reviews and delivery all happen online.
